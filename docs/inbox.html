Inbox

Triaged, explained, and already drafted

The posting says a large part of this job is the inbox. The work is not sorting mail — it is noticing that the message which looks least urgent is the one that matters. Every ranking here carries the reasoning behind it, so the judgment can be argued with rather than trusted.

Where the data comes from

On this demo

Twelve invented messages from invented senders, written to include the cases that are actually hard to call.

Running live

A read-only mail connector every ten minutes. The triage agent assigns priority and SLA, states the real ask in one line, and drafts a reply in the sender's own register. It never sends, archives, labels, or deletes.

What you can do here

  • Filter by priority or action, and search across senders, subjects, and reasoning
  • Read any message with its drafted reply side by side, and copy the draft in one click
  • 'Why it ranks here' explains every priority call, including the ones based on inference rather than evidence
  • Flags the patterns worth noticing — a second ask in five days, a broken mail-merge tag, a deadline buried in a paragraph
